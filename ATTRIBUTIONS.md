# Image Attributions

All original `nerve_*` media — every anatomy/scan clip **and** every
probe-position photo — is credited to **The POCUS Atlas**
(thepocusatlas.com), **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**. Most ultrasound clips carry a
visible "The POCUS Atlas" watermark; the probe photos are credited to the
Atlas per the project owner's instruction. (File metadata had been stripped
by a prior ezgif.com conversion, so watermarks were the only embedded
provenance signal.)

The only exceptions are the supplementary home-screen thumbnails sourced
from Wikimedia Commons / open-access journals — these are verifiably **not**
from The POCUS Atlas and retain their true attribution (detailed below).

> **Licensing action items before any non-educational / commercial use:**
> The POCUS Atlas content is **NC (non-commercial)** and **ND (no
> derivatives)**. This free Mizzou EM teaching reference is non-commercial.
> The clips were format-converted (GIF→MP4) before collection, which is in
> tension with the ND term — confirm reuse terms with The POCUS Atlas, or
> replace with original footage, before any public/commercial release.

Attribution is rendered inline in the app (caption credit per media item +
a per-block "Media" line) and recorded here for the record.

## PMC / Open-access journals

### `pmc_peng_sonoanatomy.jpg` — PENG card
- **Source:** Bilal *et al.* "Pericapsular nerve group (PENG) block — a scoping review." *Ain-Shams Journal of Anesthesiology*, 2022. [PMC8919174](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8919174/), Figure 5.
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s42077-022-00227-0
- **License:** CC-BY 4.0
- **Use:** Side-by-side probe placement photo + ultrasound sonoanatomy with labeled landmarks (AIIS, IPE, PT, FA).

### `pmc_adductor_canal_sonoanatomy.jpg` — Adductor Canal card
- **Source:** Wong *et al.* "Defining the location of the adductor canal using ultrasound." *Regional Anesthesia & Pain Medicine* 42(2):241–245, 2017. [PMC5318152](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5318152/), Figure 1.
- **License:** CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0
- **Use:** Three-level ultrasound demonstration of the adductor canal (femoral triangle → adductor canal → adductor hiatus), with labeled and unlabeled panels.
- **Restriction:** Non-commercial, no derivatives. Embed at original aspect ratio without modification. Suitable for Mizzou EM educational use; revisit licensing before any commercial deployment.
